Championship Bracket

Standard APA rules apply, with the clarifications below.

1

Object of the Game

  • Teams get solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15).
  • Clear your balls, then legally pocket the 8-ball to win.
2

Racking

  • Rack all 15 balls tightly.
  • Front ball on foot spot, 8-ball in center.
  • Back-corner balls: one solid, one stripe.
3

Breaking

  • Rock-paper-scissors decides who breaks.
  • Break behind the kitchen line; drive 4+ object balls to a rail.
  • Illegal break: re-rack and break again.
  • Scratch on the break: opponent gets ball-in-hand in the kitchen.
4

8-Ball on the Break

  • 8-ball pocketed on the break: automatic win.
  • 8-ball and cue ball pocketed on the break: automatic loss.
5

Open Table

  • Table is open until a solid or stripe is legally pocketed.
  • While open, either group may be hit first, but not the 8-ball.
6

Slop

  • Slop rules apply: only the 8-ball needs to be called.
7

Fouls

  • Pocketing the cue ball.
  • Failing to hit your object ball first.
  • No ball pocketed or driven to a rail after contact.
  • Double taps.
8

8-Ball Loss Conditions

You automatically lose if you:

  • Pocket the 8-ball before clearing your balls.
  • Pocket the 8-ball in the wrong or uncalled pocket.
  • Scratch while pocketing the 8-ball.
9

Balls Leaving the Table

  • Object ball: Returned as near the foot spot as possible.
  • 8-ball: Results in an automatic loss.
  • Cue ball: Counts as a scratch.
10

Coaching

  • Only the two teammates shooting may coach or suggest.
  • First violation: warning. Repeats: opponents get ball-in-hand.
11

Table Care

  • Keep at least one foot down while shooting.
  • Do not sit on, mark, wet, or otherwise alter the table.
  • Jump shots are allowed unless they damage the table.
