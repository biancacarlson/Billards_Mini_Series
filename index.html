  • Running late? Notify Bianca or Emily ASAP.

Rankings

  • Ties are broken by head-to-head results.

Standard APA Rules ApplySome Clarifications Below

1

Object of the Game

  • Teams get solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15).
  • Clear your group, either solids or stripes, then legally pocket the 8-ball to win.
2

Racking

  • Rack all 15 balls tightly.
  • Front ball on foot spot, 8-ball in the center.
  • Back-corner balls: One solid, one stripe.
3

Breaking

  • Rock-paper-scissors decides who breaks.
  • Break from behind the kitchen line; drive 4
    or more object balls to any rail.
  • 1st illegal break: Re-rack and break again.
  • 2nd illegal break: Opponent breaks.
  • Scratch on the break: Opponent gets ball-in-hand in the kitchen.
4

8-Ball on the Break

  • 8-ball pocketed on the break: Automatic win.
  • 8-ball and cue ball pocketed: Automatic loss.
5

Open Table

  • The table remains open until a solid or
    stripe is legally pocketed.
  • While open, either group may be hit first, but not the 8-ball.
6

Slop

  • Only the 8-ball needs to be called.
7

Fouls

  • Pocketing the cue ball.
  • Failing to hit your object ball first.
  • No ball is pocketed or driven to a rail.
  • Double taps.
8

8-Ball Conditions

An automatic loss if you:

  • Pocket the 8-ball before clearing your group.
  • Making the 8-ball in an uncalled pocket.
  • Scratch while pocketing the 8-ball.
9

Coaching

  • Only the teams playing may coach/suggest.
  • First violation: A warning.
  • Repeat: Opponents get ball-in-hand.
10

Table Care

  • At least one foot must be touching the floor.
  • Do not mark, wet, or otherwise alter table.
  • Jump shots are permitted, as long as no damage is caused to the table.
11

Balls Leaving the Table

  • Object ball: Returned as near the foot
    spot as possible.
  • 8-ball: Results in an automatic loss.
  • Cue ball: Counts as a scratch.
