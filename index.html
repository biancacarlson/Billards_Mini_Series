Championship Bracket

Standard APA rules apply, with the clarifications below.

1

Object of the Game

  • Teams are assigned solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15).
  • Win by pocketing all your balls, then legally pocketing the 8-ball.
2

Racking

  • Rack all 15 balls tightly.
  • Place the front ball on the foot spot and the 8-ball in the center.
  • The two back-corner balls must be one solid and one stripe.
3

Breaking

  • Rock-paper-scissors decides who breaks.
  • Break from behind the kitchen line & drive at least 4 object balls to a rail.
  • Illegal break? Re-rack & break again.
  • Scratch on the break? Opponent gets ball-in-hand in the kitchen.
4

8-Ball on the Break

  • Pocketing the 8-ball on the break is an automatic win.
  • Pocketing the 8-ball & cue ball on the break is an automatic loss.
5

Open Table

  • The table remains open until a player legally pockets a solid or stripe.
  • On an open table, either group may be hit first, but never the 8-ball.
6

Slop

  • Slop rules apply. Regular shots do not need to be called; the 8-ball must be called.
7

Fouls

  • Scratch (pocketing the cue ball).
  • Failure to hit your object ball first.
  • No ball pocketed & no ball reaches a rail after contact.
  • Double hit or double tap.
  • After any foul other than a break scratch, the opposing team gets ball-in-hand anywhere on the table.
8

8-Ball Loss Conditions

You lose immediately if you:

  • Pocket the 8-ball before clearing all your solids or stripes.
  • Pocket the 8-ball in an uncalled or incorrect pocket.
  • Scratch on the same shot as pocketing the 8-ball.
9

Balls Leaving the Table

  • Object ball: Spotted as close as possible to the foot spot.
  • 8-ball: Automatic loss.
  • Cue ball: Counts as a scratch.
10

Coaching

  • Only the two teammates currently shooting may coach or offer suggestions.
  • First violation: Warning. Subsequent violations: Opponent gets ball-in-hand.
11

Table Care

  • Keep at least one foot on the floor while shooting.
  • Do not sit on the table or mark, wet, or alter the cloth.
  • Jump shots are allowed as long as the table is not damaged.
