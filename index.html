Rules

Standard 8-ball with the league clarifications below. Teammates alternate turns during doubles play.

1

Object of the Game

  • Teams take solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15).
  • Win by pocketing your entire group, then legally pocketing the 8-ball in a called pocket.
2

Racking

  • Rack all 15 balls tightly.
  • Front ball goes on the foot spot; the 8-ball goes in the center.
3

Break

  • Order: Coin toss or rock-paper-scissors decides the first break. After Game 1, the previous game’s winner chooses who breaks.
  • Requirements: Break from behind the head string, hit the rack first, and drive at least 4 object balls to a rail.
  • Illegal Break: Re-rack and the same player tries again. If the second try is also illegal, the opposing team breaks.
4

8-Ball on the Break

  • Pocketing the 8-ball on the break is an automatic win.
  • If you also pocket the cue ball, the opposing team wins.
5

Open Table

  • The table stays open until a player legally pockets a solid or stripe.
  • While open, you can hit either group first, but never the 8-ball.
6

Slop

  • Slop rules apply—no need to call regular shots or pockets.
  • You must call the pocket for the 8-ball.
7

Fouls and Ball-in-Hand

  • Fouls: Scratching, failing to hit your object ball first, failing to drive a ball to a rail after legal contact (if nothing is pocketed), cue-ball movement, or double hits.
  • Break Scratch: Opposing team gets ball-in-hand behind the head string.
  • Other Fouls: Opposing team gets ball-in-hand anywhere on the table.
8

8-Ball Loss Conditions

You lose immediately if you:

  • Pocket the 8-ball before clearing your group.
  • Pocket the 8-ball in the wrong pocket.
  • Pocket the 8-ball without properly calling the pocket.
  • Scratch on the same shot as pocketing the 8-ball.
9

Jump and Masse Shots

  • Jump shots and masse shots are allowed.
10

Balls Leaving the Table

  • Pocketed balls must stay in the pocket to count.
  • Object ball off the table: Spotted as close as possible to the foot spot.
  • 8-ball off the table: Loss of game.
  • Cue ball off the table: Counts as a scratch.
11

Coaching

  • Only the two teammates currently shooting may coach or point/mark a spot on the table.
  • Penalty: Warning first, then opposing team gets ball-in-hand.
12

Shooting Position and Table Care

  • Keep at least one foot on the floor while shooting.
  • Do not sit on the table, or mark, wet, or alter the cloth.
