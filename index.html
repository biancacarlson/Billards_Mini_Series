Championship Bracket

Standard APA rules apply with some clarifications below.

1

Object of the Game

  • Teams take solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15).
  • Win by pocketing your entire group, then legally pocketing the 8-ball.
2

Racking

  • Rack all 15 balls tightly.
  • Front ball goes on the foot spot; the 8-ball goes in the center.
  • The two balls in the back corners of the rack must be one solid and one stripe.
3

Break

  • Order: Rock-paper-scissors decides who breaks.
  • Requirements: Break from behind the kitchen line, drive at least 4 object balls to a rail.
  • Illegal Break: If the requirements aren’t met, re-rack and break again.
  • Break Scratch: Opponent places cue ball in the kitchen.
4

8-Ball on the Break

  • Pocketing the 8-ball on the break is an automatic win.
  • If you also pocket the cue ball, the opposing team wins.
5

Open Table

  • The table stays open until a player legally pockets a solid or stripe.
  • While open, you can hit either group first, but never the 8-ball.
6

Slop

  • Slop rules apply (no need to call regular shots or pockets, except for the 8-ball).
7

Fouls and Ball-in-Hand

  • Scratching (pocketing the cue ball).
  • Not hitting your object ball first.
  • Nothing pocketed and no ball driven to a rail after legal contact.
  • Double taps.
  • Ball-in-Hand: After any foul besides a break scratch, the opposing team places the cue ball anywhere on the table.
8

8-Ball Loss Conditions

You lose immediately if you:

  • Pocket the 8-ball before clearing your group.
  • Pocket the 8-ball in the wrong pocket or without calling it.
  • Scratch on the same shot as pocketing the 8-ball.
9

Jump and Masse Shots

  • Both are allowed.
10

Balls Leaving the Table

  • Object ball off the table: Spotted as close as possible to the foot spot.
  • 8-ball off the table: Loss of game.
  • Cue ball off the table: Counts as a scratch.
11

Coaching

  • Only the two teammates currently shooting may coach/ provide suggestions.
  • Penalty: Warning first, then opposing team gets ball-in-hand.
12

Positioning & Table Care

  • Keep at least one foot on the floor while shooting.
  • Do not sit on the table, or mark, wet, or alter the cloth.
