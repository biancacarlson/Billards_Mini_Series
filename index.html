Match Format

  • Round Robin: Every team plays every other team once.
  • Three games are played each night, except for one week when teams play two.
  • Each game counts as one win or one loss toward the standings.

Arrival Times

  • 10 minutes late: Your team forfeits its first game.
  • 30 minutes late: Your team forfeits its first two games.
  • Running late? Notify Bianca or Emily as early as possible.

Rankings

  • Game Wins: Teams are ranked by their total number of games won.
  • Head-to-Head: If teams are tied, their results against each other determine the ranking.

Playoffs

  • The top 8 teams advance to the playoffs after Week 4.
  • Quarterfinals and the 3rd-place match are single elimination.
  • Semifinals and the championship are best-of-three.
  • Winning the first two games eliminates the need for a third game.

Championship Bracket

Standard APA rules apply, with the clarifications below.

1

Object of the Game

  • Each team is assigned either solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15).
  • Win by pocketing all your team’s balls, then legally pocketing the 8-ball.
2

Racking

  • Rack all 15 balls tightly.
  • Place the front ball on the foot spot, with the 8-ball in the center.
  • The two back-corner balls must consist of one solid and one stripe.
3

Breaking

  • Rock-paper-scissors determines who breaks.
  • Break from behind the kitchen line and drive at least four object balls to a rail.
  • If the break is illegal, re-rack and break again.
  • If you scratch on the break, your opponent gets ball-in-hand in the kitchen.
4

8-Ball on the Break

  • Pocketing the 8-ball on the break results in an automatic win.
  • Pocketing both the 8-ball and cue ball on the break results in an automatic loss.
5

Open Table

  • The table remains open until a player legally pockets a solid or stripe.
  • While the table is open, either group may be contacted first, but the 8-ball cannot be contacted first.
6

Slop

  • Slop rules apply. Regular shots do not need to be called, but the 8-ball must be called.
7

Fouls

  • Pocketing the cue ball.
  • Failing to hit your object ball first.
  • Failing to pocket a ball or drive one to a rail after contact.
  • Double taps.
8

8-Ball Loss Conditions

You automatically lose if you:

  • Pocket the 8-ball before clearing all your assigned balls.
  • Pocket the 8-ball in the wrong pocket or without calling the pocket.
  • Scratch on the same shot as pocketing the 8-ball.
9

Balls Leaving the Table

  • Object ball: Returned to the table as close to the foot spot as possible.
  • 8-ball: Results in an automatic loss.
  • Cue ball: Counts as a scratch.
10

Coaching

  • Only the two teammates currently shooting may offer coaching or suggestions.
  • First violation results in a warning. Subsequent violations give the opposing team ball-in-hand.
11

Table Care

  • Keep at least one foot on the floor while shooting.
  • Do not sit on the table, mark the cloth, wet it, or otherwise alter it.
  • Jump shots are allowed as long as they do not damage the table.
